# LS Salon: Features Overview For Marketing

Use this document as a simple feature and functionality reference for marketing collateral, website copy, brochures, sales decks, and product summaries.

## Product Summary

LS Salon is a multi-tenant salon and wellness business management platform designed for single locations, multi-branch brands, and parent companies managing multiple salons under one umbrella.

It combines operations, scheduling, team management, inventory, reporting, and multi-location administration in one platform.

## Ideal Customer Profiles

LS Salon is suitable for:
- independent salons
- growing salon chains
- wellness and beauty brands with multiple outlets
- parent companies managing several salon businesses
- operational teams that need role-based control across multiple locations

## Core Product Positioning

LS Salon helps salon businesses:
- centralize day-to-day operations
- manage multiple branches from one platform
- streamline appointments and staff scheduling
- control inventory and purchases
- manage employee leave and availability
- improve visibility with reports and audit trails

## Key Feature Areas

## 1. Multi-Tenant Salon SaaS Architecture

- Each salon can operate as its own separate account and workspace.
- Multiple salons can run on the same platform while keeping their business data separated.
- The platform supports both single-salon and multi-salon business models.
- Tenants can use default subdomain access or custom branded domains.

## 2. Parent Company / Multi-Branch Administration

- A parent company can manage multiple salon tenants under one umbrella.
- Organizations can group multiple salon accounts together.
- Parent-company users can access only the salons and data assigned to their organization.
- Platform super admins can oversee the full structure across all organizations and tenants.

## 3. Role-Based Access Control

- Different user roles are supported for different operational needs.
- Platform admins can manage high-level provisioning and control.
- Organization users can be assigned owner, admin, or viewer permissions.
- Tenant-level roles support business operations such as admin, manager, staff, and customer access models.

## 4. Appointment Management

- Manage salon appointments in a dedicated scheduling workflow.
- Create, edit, reschedule, and manage service bookings.
- Support service-based booking flows tied to staff availability.
- Built-in coupon support for appointment-related promotions and offers.

## 5. Service Management

- Create and organize salon services in a structured catalog.
- Group services into categories for easier discovery and administration.
- Support pricing and service configuration for operational consistency.
- Support package-style services in addition to standard services.

## 6. Staff and User Management

- Manage salon users from a centralized interface.
- Maintain staff, managers, customers, and administrative users.
- Invite and onboard users into the system.
- Track role-based access and user status management.

## 7. Shift Scheduling and Workforce Planning

- Create reusable shift templates.
- Manage staff schedules across days and weeks.
- Support roster views for operational planning.
- Support recurring schedule patterns for teams with repeating availability.

## 8. Leave Management

- Create leave definitions and leave policies.
- Manage leave groups and approvals.
- Allow staff to submit leave requests.
- Help managers coordinate staff availability with operational planning.

## 9. Inventory and Purchase Management

- Track salon products and inventory items.
- Organize products into categories.
- Manage supplier records.
- Record and manage purchases.
- Improve operational control over stock movement and replenishment.

## 10. Reports and Operational Visibility

- Access reporting tools for business monitoring.
- Review coupon usage reporting.
- Track audit logs for administrative transparency.
- Support better oversight for operations and change history.

## 11. Settings and Business Configuration

- Configure general operational settings.
- Manage tax-related settings.
- Maintain business rules and setup from a central settings area.

## 12. Security and Administrative Controls

- Tenant data is separated within a shared platform architecture.
- Platform provisioning actions are restricted to the right administrative roles.
- Password reset and account recovery workflows are built in.
- Audit logging supports traceability for important administrative actions.

## 13. Branding and Access Flexibility

- Support branded tenant access using custom domains.
- Allow salon businesses to use platform-managed URLs or their own branded web addresses.
- Enable a more premium branded experience for growing salon groups.

## Business Benefits

LS Salon can help businesses:
- reduce operational fragmentation
- improve appointment and staff coordination
- standardize processes across locations
- support growth from one salon to multiple branches
- give parent companies better visibility and control
- provide a more professional branded digital experience
