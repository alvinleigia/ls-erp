# P12 partial and milestone commercial billing

This extends P11 through the same invoice, payment, document, access and audit
services. The accepted order remains the commercial agreement and its existing
receivable remains the debt. Billing allocations never create a second ledger.
This is common across industries; Real Estate supplies its accepted source facts
and milestone labels through the existing adapters.

## Creating an invoice

Open a confirmed Sales Order and its Commercial Invoices section. The order-level
customer statement shows the agreed value, net posted invoices, credits, unbilled
value, draft reservations and value available for another invoice. Payment figures
are shown only with the independent application/payment Read permissions.

Select **Create invoice draft** and choose:

| Bill by | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Remaining available balance | Invoice the remainder after posted invoices and other drafts. |
| Full accepted order | Invoice the whole agreement; available only when its full value remains available. |
| Fixed amount | Invoice a specified amount in the agreement's currency. |
| Percentage of accepted order | Apply the percentage to the original agreed total, not the remaining balance. |
| Accepted payment-plan milestone | Use the remaining accepted payable amount for a saved plan position. Open receivables first. |
| Selected line quantities | Invoice selected remaining billing quantities from accepted lines. |

Add a billing description, invoice date, optional due date and customer note.
Save the draft, review its calculated amount and line allocation, then post it.
Posting freezes the allocation and assigns the ordinary invoice number. Draft
date/note edits preserve the saved allocation; changing the billing selection
explicitly recalculates it. A balance draft never grows merely because another
invoice was credited. Use a new draft or an explicit amount for additional value.

## Quantities and rounding

The exact accepted total, including agreed discounts and charges, is allocated
proportionately across its saved lines. Fixed amounts, percentages and milestones
consume equivalent billing quantities. Selected quantities use the remaining
accepted line value; a final portion absorbs currency rounding. Mixing billing
methods therefore cannot bill the same agreed value or quantity twice.

These are billing shares, not delivery records or inventory movements. No service
completion or construction progress is certified automatically. Invoicing a
milestone does not establish that the milestone was completed.

The database owns allocation and rounding. Drafts reserve their shares, so parallel
writers cannot overbill. Cancelling a draft releases its share. Full credit of an
individual posted invoice releases only that invoice's value and quantities.
Other invoices remain posted. History, numbers and issued PDFs remain intact.

## Advances, payments and statements

Payments already recorded on the order can be applied across its posted invoices.
The combined active applications cannot exceed either the payment or each invoice.
No new payment is created. Crediting an invoice releases its applications into the
order's unapplied balance; it does not refund money, cancel the order or change its
agreed value. Corrections/refunds remain independent recorded-payment operations.

The customer statement is per order, not a consolidated statement across every
agreement for a customer. It separates received payments, invoice applications,
unapplied advances, posted-invoice outstanding and the canonical order balance.
For example, an advance paid before any invoicing is visible as unapplied until
explicitly applied; it does not silently reduce the amount agreed or billed.

## Documents and access

Existing company templates and the shared editor continue to work. Invoice totals
and required pricing sections show this invoice's billed share; the full agreement
is labelled as reference information. Full credit-note PDFs use that invoice's
amount. New issuance remains immutable and replacements remain linked. Historical
downloads return the exact previously issued file, with acknowledgement after
crediting; no historical PDF is regenerated by this migration.

All lists, forms, transitions, applications, statements and PDF operations inherit
the canonical order's tenant, company/office, sharing and Restricted boundaries.
Profiles, actions, parent Read and current module activation remain mandatory.
The module adds no grants, activation, staff/manager fallbacks or tenant overrides.

## Migration and scope

Migration `20261030000300_partial_billing` adds the frozen `CustomerInvoice.billing`
allocation. Existing invoices receive their original full-order allocation, with
all prior columns, timestamps, source snapshots, numbers, events, applications and
PDFs preserved. It replaces the single-active-invoice index with aggregate value
and quantity guards; four existing transition/document guards now use the frozen
invoice amount. A deferred native proof also bounds writers waiting on a lock.

There is no runtime historical fallback. The allocation is separate from accepted
source evidence, allowing a future statutory-tax adapter to freeze its own tax
facts without repurposing commercial values. GST, statutory credit/debit notes,
e-invoicing, general-ledger postings, delivery-based certification, automatic
milestone triggering and partial credit within one invoice remain outside P12.
Use full credit of the affected partial invoice for current corrections.

Run `npm.cmd run test:customer-invoices` for migration preservation and invoice,
payment, order, ownership, storage and recovery regressions. Browser flows cover
full and partial billing, advance sharing, statements and immutable PDF history.
